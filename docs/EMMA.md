## Log in

1. Open the link above
2. Sign in with the Sanity account you were invited to
3. If you don’t have an account, use **Sign up** with the email Fiona invited

---

## Update the menu

1. Click **Menu product** in the left sidebar
2. Click an existing product to edit, or **Create new** for a new item
3. Change the photo, title, caption, description, or ingredients
4. **Where it appears** controls whether it shows as a big photo card or only in the full menu list
5. **Sort order** — lower numbers appear first (1 before 2)
6. Click **Publish** (green button, top right)
7. Open the live website and **refresh** to see changes

### Remove a product

Open the product → **Unpublish** or **Delete** → confirm → refresh the website.

---

## Update photos on the homepage

1. Click **Site images** in the sidebar
2. **Hero images** — big photos at the top of the site
3. **In the wild** — grid of lifestyle photos further down
4. Add, remove, or reorder images. Each needs **Alt text** (short description of the photo)
5. Click **Publish**
6. Refresh the live website

---

## Live website

**https://theshakeshed.vercel.app**
